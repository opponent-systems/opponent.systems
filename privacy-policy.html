Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Opponent Systems Inc. ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and retain information when you interact with our services, including communications sent or received through our Twilio integration.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our messaging or voice services, we may collect the following types of information:

Contact Information

  • Phone numbers used to send or receive messages

Communication Data

  • SMS or MMS message content
  • Message delivery status and timestamps

Technical Metadata

  • Message logs
  • Twilio message identifiers

This data may be processed through Twilio Inc., our communications service provider.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Deliver SMS and MMS
  • Respond to inquiries
  • Monitor system performance and reliability
  • Maintain records of communications
  • Improve our services and integrations

We do not sell personal data collected through our communications systems.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use Twilio Inc. to power messaging and voice communications.

Information transmitted through Twilio is subject to Twilio's infrastructure and processing policies.

Twilio's privacy policy can be found at: https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy

5. Data Retention

We retain communication data only for as long as necessary to operate our services and meet legal or operational requirements.

Typical retention may include:

  • Message logs and metadata: 30 days from the last message sent from user to us, or until user opts out of communication by messaging STOP or sending a request to privacy@opponent.systems.
  • Message content: 30 days from the last message sent from user to us, or until user opts out of communication by messaging STOP or sending a request to privacy@opponent.systems.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: privacy@opponent.systems

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will be posted at this URL with the revised "Last updated" date.
